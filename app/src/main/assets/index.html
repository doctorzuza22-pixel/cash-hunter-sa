Before you claim

1. Confirm it's meant for youCheck the sender, recipient details and bank notification. A found or forwarded PIN does not prove ownership.
2. Check expiry with the bankExpired or uncollected transfers may be reversed to the sender. Ask the bank or sender to confirm the status.
3. Use official recovery channelsIf your own voucher is missing, contact the bank through its official app, website or branch. Never pay a stranger to release a voucher.

Important safety rules

A search result is not proof that you own the money. Only the fund, bank, insurer or lawful administrator can verify entitlement and approve payment. Never use another person's voucher PIN, OTP or banking credentials. Do not pay an upfront fee to someone promising guaranteed recovery.
